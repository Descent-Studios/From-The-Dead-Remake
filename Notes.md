Player : 
- Actual character model [ ]
- SFX for walking[x], hurting, idle?
- Implementation of rage and exhaustion 
	- Rage
		- Rage increases with level time and hurt damage
		- Rage decreases with package delivery
		- If rage hits max, player enters enraged mode
	- Enraged
		- Player keeps rage up by delivering and hitting zombies
		- Rage bar burns out
		- Increased damage, speed and throwing strength
		- If rage depletes, player is exhausted
	- Exhaustion
		- Player is slowed down, less strength
		- Rage bar max decreases
		- Player needs to do damage or deliver packages to bring the bar back to normal
